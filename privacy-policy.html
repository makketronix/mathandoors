Privacy Policy: Math And Doors

App: Math And Doors (Android, package com.makketronics.mathanddoors) Developer: Makketronix ("we", "us") Effective date: October 24, 2026 Contact: [CONTACT EMAIL]

Math And Doors is a learning game for young children: they answer simple math questions or sort shapes and pictures by size to walk through doors. We built it to collect nothing. This policy explains what that means.

The short version

  • The app does not collect, use, store on any server, share, or sell any personal or device information.
  • There are no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no tracking of any kind.
  • The app requests no Android permissions, including no internet permission. It works fully offline and cannot send anything anywhere.
  • Makketronix runs no server for this app. We never see who plays, how they play, or what they tap.
  • A few game settings are saved only on the device, and uninstalling the app deletes them.

What the app saves on the device

To remember preferences between sessions, the app saves a small list of settings in its own private storage on the device:

  • which game was played last (Math or Sorting) and the math mode (+, −, or both)
  • which doors appear, the reward door, and the celebration (magic hall or fireworks, and after how many doors)
  • whether counting dots, sound effects, music, and reading aloud are on

Game progress (doors reached and stars) is not saved: every game starts fresh.

These settings never leave the device. The app turns off Android's automatic cloud backup, so they are not copied to a Google account either. We cannot see or access them. To erase them, uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android Settings > Apps > Math And Doors > Storage.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for names, email addresses, ages, birthdays, photos, voice recordings, location, or contacts.
  • It does not use the camera, the microphone, or location.
  • It does not use advertising IDs, device identifiers, cookies, or fingerprinting.
  • It contains no ads, no third-party SDKs that collect data, no links out of the app, and no social features.
  • It has no in-app purchases.

Reading aloud (text-to-speech)

The app can read each task out loud ("What is 3 plus 5?") and say short, fixed phrases such as "Great job!". For this it uses the text-to-speech engine already installed on the device (for example Speech Services by Google), and it only chooses voices that work offline.

The only text the app gives to the speech engine is the task and its fixed phrases, never personal information. The speech engine is part of the device and follows its maker's privacy policy. Reading aloud can be turned off in the app's Settings (Read questions aloud), or all sound with Sound effects.

Google Play

The app is downloaded through Google Play. Google operates Google Play and may process information about downloads, installs, and your device under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Google gives developers only aggregated statistics (such as the number of installs by country), which we do not use to identify anyone.

Children's privacy

Math And Doors is made for children. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. This is consistent with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code, and with Google Play's Families Policy.

Your rights

We hold no personal information about you or your child, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete. To remove the settings saved on the device, uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how it handles information, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released. We will not start collecting personal information without saying so clearly here first.
